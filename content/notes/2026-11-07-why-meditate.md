---
title: Why meditate?
date: 2026-11-07
---
I knew meditation worked.

But I still couldn’t get myself to do it.

I had read the studies. Meditation is a proven path to a calm, resilient mind. I knew from the great thinkers, the saints and the sages, that silence is where peace lives.

It didn’t matter.

There was always too much to do. Meditation fell last on my priority list, behind work, family, and health. And so for years my practice stayed inconsistent, and nothing changed.

**But then I ran an experiment.**

I meditated, and I meant it this time. I took my time. I resisted the urge to scratch my nose or check the clock. Twenty minutes of silence and stillness, twice a day. Sometimes as long as an hour.

Over the following weeks and months, I noticed subtle changes within my mind.

I grew calmer. Less anxious. More Alive! Work stopped feeling as hard as it once did. I felt I could take on bigger challenges. As I became aware of the voice in my head, I changed how I interpreted the world around me. Life began to happen for me rather than to me. Beauty surrounded me everywhere I looked.

Here’s what I missed for years.

**It was never about the meditation. It’s about the person you become when you commit to understanding yourself.** You set aside time every day, away from the noise of the world and you return able to see it with wonder and clarity.

I’m not perfect. I still rush a session or shorten it. But I meditate every day now.

It’s my number one priority.

If you overthink the way I do, run your own experiment. Twenty minutes a day. No guidance, no apps. Just you, still, in a quiet room. You may be surprised by what comes up from the deep recesses of your mind.
