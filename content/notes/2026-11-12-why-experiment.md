---
title: Why experiment?
date: 2026-11-12
---
Most of us expect to get big decisions right on the first try.

- We hope our first crush will be the one.
- We hope our first job will become our life’s work.
- We hope the first city we move to will be home for good.

But that is rarely how it works. Big decisions are complex, and your first attempt is unlikely to be your best. You don’t know enough yet. And you can’t know enough until you try.

That is why experiments matter. Instead of guessing the right answer, break the big decision into small tests you can learn from. Some tests will work out and many will fail, but the mistakes often teach you the most.

So don’t fear your mistakes. **The faster you learn what is wrong, the sooner you find what is right.**

And nobody can make these mistakes for you. Some things have to be lived to be understood. You can read the opinion of every expert in a field. But **the only way to make progress in your own life is to experiment.**
