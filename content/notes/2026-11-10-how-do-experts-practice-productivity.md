---
title: Why should your to-do list have only six things?
date: 2026-11-10
topic: Attention
---
Study the best in any field (scientists, entrepreneurs, artists, athletes, leaders) and you’ll find one trait they all share:

**Focus.**

They’ve learned to master their attention and point it at the few levers that drive their output.

The Ivy Lee method is a system to do the same in six simple steps:

1. At the end of each workday, write down the six most important things you need to do tomorrow. No more than six.
2. Rank them in order of importance.
3. Tomorrow, work only on the first task.
4. Finish it before you move to the next one.
5. Move any unfinished tasks to a new list of six for the next day.
6. Repeat every workday.

This method works because it is simple. You know exactly what to do and when to do it.

- Because you made the tough decision the night before, you start the next day with no friction.
- Because you can only pick six, you are forced to choose what matters most.
- Because you work in order, you focus on one task at a time.

When an emergency comes up, deal with it, then return to your list as soon as you can.

**The key is to give your full attention to the most important thing first each day.** It’s the only productivity hack you need.
