---
title: How do you use spirituality to deal with pain?
date: 2026-11-25
---
**Use your pain to explore your awareness.**

Let yourself feel the pain without resisting it. Keep asking, *“Who is the one who notices the pain?”* A spiritual being honors what the mind, heart and body are going through.

Remember, **you are not your pain. You are the one who is aware of it.**

You can remain aware of your pain and still create some distance, resting in your centered awareness. Relax, breathe, and offer your suffering to God. Imagine reaching your inner hands up way over your head and releasing the pain into the universe.

This is how a spiritual being goes through pain consciously. They use their pain to evolve spiritually.
