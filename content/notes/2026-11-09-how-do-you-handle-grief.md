---
title: How do you handle grief?
date: 2026-11-09
---
There is nothing wrong with feeling sorrow.

**It means you loved deeply.**

The problem starts when you fight your emotions or cling to them. Resist sorrow, and it gets stuck. Hold on to it, and it leaves scars.

Think of your emotions as an orchestra. Some parts are uplifting, and some are solemn. The symphony only comes together when you let every part play. Grief is one of the solemn parts.

When it rises, step back and give it some space. Tell yourself, *“I can handle this,”* and relax. Let it come, and let it pass.

You don’t need to close down or push the grief away. **You need to open up and let it move through you.**

In that openness, you will find a deeper connection to the one you lost, and to the source of love itself.
