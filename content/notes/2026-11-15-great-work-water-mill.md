**This is not a photograph. This is an oil painting.**

Frits Thaulow painted *Water Mill* in 1892. What’s remarkable is how alive the water feels.

Notice the light shining through the river onto the stones on the bottom, the pale reflections of the buildings on the surface, and the ripples running through the green-tinged water. The river almost seems to move.

Thaulow was born in Norway in 1847, a country of fjords, rivers, and waterfalls. He could have painted anything, yet he spent his life mastering the one subject that never holds still long enough to take shape. No one has ever captured the beauty of water in motion quite like him.

**This is Great Work: an attempt at the impossible, a rational approach to a delusional aim.**
