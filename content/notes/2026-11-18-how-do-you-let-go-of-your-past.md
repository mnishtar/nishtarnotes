---
title: How do you let go of your past?
date: 2026-11-18
---
**Never be ashamed of feeling shame.**

It’s the only way to let go of your past.

Why? Because past impressions get stuck in your heart and mind, and shame is negative energy from your past finally trying to leave.

Most of us are afraid of shame. We push it back down, and it stays stuck inside.

Don’t.

When shame comes, welcome it. Relax through it, and give it the time and space it needs to pass through. Let it go with compassion. Offer it up to a greater power.

**Shame is what stands between you and God.**
