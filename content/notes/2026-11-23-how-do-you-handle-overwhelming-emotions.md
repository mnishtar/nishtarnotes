---
title: How do you handle overwhelming emotions?
date: 2026-11-23
---
**You don’t learn to handle emotions when they’re overwhelming.**

You learn to handle them every single day leading up to that moment.

Life will present you with little challenges, annoyances, and frustrations. Your job is to practice letting go of these small things, so that when the big things come, you’ll know exactly what to do.

Think of it like swimming in the ocean. Nobody learns to swim in the middle of a storm. You practice in calm water, so when the big wave comes, you know how to stay afloat.

Here’s the practice:

**First, notice.**

Watch your emotions as they rise to a crescendo. Watch your mind as it starts rattling off, holding onto the emotion and spinning stories about it. Your mind will try to convince you that you must do something about these feelings, and you must do it now. But you don’t have to. You only have to let them pass through.

**Second, don’t identify with your emotions.**

You are not your emotions. You are the one who is aware of your emotions. They feel overwhelming because you have made them personal. But emotions are just passing energy. When you stop making them personal, they come and go just like everything else in life.

**Third, relax. Return your attention to your breath.**

If you get pulled under, don’t judge yourself. Come back as soon as you notice. Instead of getting lost in your emotions, you stay behind them as an observer, not a participant.

This process takes time. But if you commit to practicing with the small frustrations of everyday life, you’ll find that even the strongest emotions can come without taking you down.
