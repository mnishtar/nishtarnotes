---
title: How do you meet your future self?
date: 2026-11-17
---
Your future self is waiting for you. They have mastered the skills, beliefs, and traits you want today.

**But you will only meet them if you believe they exist.**

Your brain will find information in your environment to support whatever you believe.

If you think something is impossible, you quit, and it stays impossible. If you think it is possible, you keep showing up long enough to find out. Over time, what you believe becomes your reality.

That is why your words matter so much.

Instead of saying you lack something, say it hasn’t happened yet.

- *“I can’t focus for more than 20 minutes yet.”*
- *“I don’t know how to learn well yet.”*
- *“I can’t prioritize my to-do list yet.”*

“Yet” is a word of possibility. It is the path to your future self, the one who has mastered what you are working on now.

Visualize the person you will be 10 years from now. What skills, beliefs, and traits do they possess? How does it feel to be that version of you?

If you show up day after day, and love yourself enough to learn and practice the fundamentals, then one day you will meet them.

**That is the power of “yet.”**
