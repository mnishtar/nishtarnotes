---
title: How do you reclaim your attention?
date: 2026-11-19
topic: Attention
---
**Your brain craves novelty.**

Whenever you’re surprised or exposed to something new, your brain releases a little drip of the “reward” neurotransmitter dopamine.

Social media companies have used this knowledge to make their platforms irresistible. Every buzz and ping creates anticipation, and checking your notifications instantly relieves it.

Over time, your brain learns to crave the quick and easy hits of distraction rather than the slow, steady, and satisfying work that requires your full attention.

So reading, writing, and meaningful conversations feel harder than they should.

But you can choose to reclaim your attention.

**This means doing one thing at a time, with intention and attention.**

When it’s time to read, write, exercise, meditate, or connect with your family, set your devices aside:

- Use a website blocker.
- Put your phone on “do not disturb.”
- Switch your screen to grayscale.

With practice, you will reclaim your attention bit by bit.

And you will rewire your brain to crave deep, satisfying work.
