---
title: What does it mean to process painful emotions?
date: 2026-11-16
---
**You’ve worked through emotional pain when you no longer have to think about it.**

There was a time when certain thoughts, memories, or experiences would set you off. Now they don’t.

Imagine driving home from work and realizing something happened today that used to bother you. This time, it didn’t. That’s how you know you’ve processed it.

Think about the toy from your childhood you were obsessed with. For me, it was my Nintendo Game Boy. Eventually, I outgrew it. I didn’t decide to move on. I just stopped reaching for it.

Emotional pain works the same way. You don’t suppress it. You don’t resist it. You let it go, and it becomes like any other passing experience.

**What once held your attention no longer holds you.** You don’t have to think about it anymore.
