---
title: How do you prime your mind for peak performance?
date: 2026-11-26
---
**A busy mind produces mediocrity. A calm mind produces excellence.**

When your head spins with endless to-do lists and competing desires, your body floods with cortisol, adrenaline, and noradrenaline. This sympathetic overdrive leaves you anxious, irritable, and distracted. It also impairs your prefrontal cortex, the part of your brain that plans and makes decisions.

**To restore your attention, slow your breath.**

When you exhale slowly, you activate your vagus nerve. This shifts your body into a calm, parasympathetic state. Your emotions settle and your thinking clears, so your attention can stay on the task in front of you.

You can create space for calm in just a few minutes:

1. Find a quiet place to sit.
2. Close your eyes and stay still.
3. Put all your attention on your breath.
4. Breathe in deeply. Then exhale slowly and feel your mind and body slow down.
5. As you open your eyes, reflect on the awareness behind the breath.

Notice how you feel. Your mind is quieter, and your focus is sharper.

Carry that calm into your next important meeting, deep work session, or big decision. Deep breath in. Slow exhale out.

**You are primed for peak performance.**
