---
title: How do you release buried pain?
date: 2026-11-11
---
You don’t need to dig it up, because digging only tightens its grip.

Instead, life brings buried pain to the surface when the time is right.

Think of fruit ripening on a tree. Pick it too soon, and you have to ripen it yourself. You’re forcing a process that works best on its own.

Your pain is no different. It releases when it’s ready, not when you force it.

So your job isn’t to go looking for it.

**It’s to be ready when it comes up on its own.**

That means building the clarity and strength to handle it. Then, when it rises, stay calm and return to your breath. Let it pass through you without getting pulled in.

**Don’t resist the pain. Relax and let it go.**
