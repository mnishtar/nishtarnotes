---
title: How do you make better decisions without more willpower?
date: 2026-10-29
---
You tell yourself you’ll stop checking your phone first thing in the morning. Then you wake up, reach for the nightstand, and you’re scrolling before your feet touch the ground.

So you blame yourself.

You promise to be more disciplined tomorrow. And tomorrow, it happens again.

**The problem isn’t your willpower. It’s your environment.**

Most of what you do each day isn’t a conscious decision, but a reaction to whatever is easiest in front of you. Every space you live in has a default (the most convenient option), and you tend to take it without noticing.

Defaults shape even big decisions. In Europe, countries that made organ donation the default had consent rates several times higher than countries that asked people to opt in. The only real difference was the option they started with.

Your mornings work the same way. As long as your phone rests beside you, reaching for it will be your default.

**Do not fight your environment. Design your defaults instead.**

Put what you want within reach and what you don’t want out of sight, because the easiest choice is the one you will make most often.

Start tonight by charging your phone in another room, and notice how different tomorrow morning feels.
