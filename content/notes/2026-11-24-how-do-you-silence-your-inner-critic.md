---
title: How do you silence your inner critic?
date: 2026-11-24
---
**You are not your inner critic.**

But it’s easy to believe you are. The voice sounds like the truth. It tries to protect you from fear, failure, and disappointment. But left unchecked, your inner critic prevents you from growing, taking chances, and fully living.

- *“You’re lazy.”*
- *“You’re so dumb.”*
- *“You’re not good enough.”*

These voices play in the background all day long. Over time they influence what you believe, how you behave, and who you become.

**To silence the voices, name them.**

When you catch your inner critic talking, give it a name that fits the feeling, like “Negative Nancy” or “Skeptical Steve.”

A name puts space between the voice and you.

In a [2007 UCLA brain-scan study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17576282/), putting a feeling into words calmed the amygdala, your brain’s emotion center. At the same time, it activated part of the prefrontal cortex, which helps keep emotions in check.

Each time you name the voice, you reclaim your agency. And you remember: you are not your inner critic.
