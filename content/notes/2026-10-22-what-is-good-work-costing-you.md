---
title: What is good work costing you?
date: 2026-10-22
---
There will always be one more thing worth doing.

That’s why so many of us never get to our Great Work.

As a father, physician, and entrepreneur, I’ve battled busyness for years. There’s always an urgent task waiting or an exciting idea pulling at me. At one point I was overwhelmed. Every project fought for the same time and energy.

Then I came across a quote from gardener Elizabeth Roth:

> “Roses that are left unpruned can become a tangled mess of old and new canes all competing for air and light.”

In other words, a rose bush can only feed so many buds. Our days are the same. Without pruning, good projects crowd out the great ones, and nothing gets the undivided attention it needs.

**You have to prune the good to make room for the great.**

The hard part is that the things you cut are good.

- If you’re a parent, your child could do soccer, piano, swimming, and coding. Each one is good for them. *Which one could they master if you cut the rest?*
- If you’re a writer, there are dozens of authors worth learning from. You could skim them all or study one line by line. *Which one would teach you the most?*
- If you’re building a business, you could ship a new feature, chase a partnership, or start a podcast. All three would help. *Which one would you bet the next six months on?*

So the next time one more good thing shows up, ask one question first:

**What will this cost my Great Work?**
