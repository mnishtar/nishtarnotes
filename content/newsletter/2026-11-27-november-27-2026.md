## 1 Quote From Others

Ralph Waldo Emerson:

"Every man I meet is my master in some point, and in that I learn of him."

*Source: Letters and Social Aims*

Reflection: A reminder to listen and learn from everyone you come across.

## 3 Ideas From Me

I.

"To become the best at what you do, find work that is a full expression of you."

---

II.

"You struggle to master new skills because you've made consistency too difficult.

- Work 40 minutes daily, not 4 hours sporadically.
- Exercise 20 minutes daily, not 2 hours sporadically.
- Meditate 2 minutes daily, not 20 minutes sporadically.

Before you can master the finer details of a skill, you must first master the basic skill of showing up."

---

III.

"You don't have to quiet your mind.<br>
You can simply watch it without letting it run your life."
