## 1 Quote From Others

Albert Einstein, as recalled by Arnold Sommerfeld:
"Since the mathematicians have invaded the theory of relativity, I do not understand it myself any more."

*Source: Albert Einstein: Philosopher-Scientist*

Reflection: Your subconscious mind is infinitely greater than your conscious mind. Imagination, not logic, is what produces Great Work.
