## 1 Quote From Others

**Ray Dalio**:

"Making the most of your circumstances is what life is all about."

*Source: Principles: Life and Work*

**Reflection:** Treat each moment as sacred. Do not spurn it with distraction. Cherish it as you would a child. Raise each moment, regardless of your preference. In time you will feel lighter as you align your choices with your circumstances.
