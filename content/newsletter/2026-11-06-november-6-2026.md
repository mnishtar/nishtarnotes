## 1 Quote From Others

**Alain de Botton on Socrates**:

"The truth, in so far as a human being is able to attain such a thing, lies in a statement which it seems impossible to disprove. It is by finding out what something is not that one comes closest to understanding what it is."

*Source: The Consolations of Philosophy*

**Reflection:**

To learn what to do, figure out what not to do.<br>
To learn who you are, figure out who you're not.<br>
To learn what you want, figure out what you don't want.

When in doubt, invert the problem.
