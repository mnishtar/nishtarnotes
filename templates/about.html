How My Work Will Benefit You

One question drives everything I write. How do you reclaim your attention so you can find and do your Great Work?

I answer it with two tools most writers keep apart. One is the science I use every day as a physician. The other is the contemplative practice I keep every day as a person.

My writing focuses on topics like…

  • How to protect your attention in a world built to take it
  • How to find the work that is truly yours
  • How meditation, sleep, exercise and diet power everything else
  • How to get very good at hard things
  • How to build healthy habits with the people you love

Most of all, I write about how to put these ideas into practice in daily life.

The Great Work Experiment

When I studied for my pain medicine boards, I used a simple method. One primary source. One secondary source. Daily review with spaced repetition. Full immersion, no shortcuts. It took me to the 99th percentile.

This site is where I test that method on bigger questions. Can the rules that worked for one exam help you find your life's work, train a calmer mind, or get a whole family moving? I run the experiment in public, one Note at a time.
